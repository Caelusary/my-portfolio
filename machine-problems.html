Machine Problems

Three machine problems from my coursework, rebuilt to run right here. Change the numbers and press Run.

01 FCFS 02 FCFS vs SJF 03 Banker's Algorithm
MP 01

FCFS CPU Scheduling

Jul 27 – Aug 10, 2026

First Come, First Served: each process runs to completion in the order it arrived. The program finds every process's turnaround and waiting time, then the averages.

Node.js Vanilla JS

Rules

  • 3 to 10 processes
  • Unique process IDs
  • Unique arrival times
  • Whole numbers only, with a burst of at least 1
MP 02

FCFS vs SJF Scheduling

Aug 25 – Sep 11, 2026

The same input under two non-preemptive schedulers. Shortest Job First picks the shortest burst among the processes that have arrived, and ties go to the earlier arrival. Compare puts both side by side.

Node.js Vanilla JS

Rules

  • 3 to 10 processes
  • Unique process IDs
  • Unique arrival times
  • Whole numbers only, with a burst of at least 1
MP 03

Banker's Algorithm

Sep 18 – Oct 1, 2026

Deadlock avoidance with one resource type. From each process's maximum need and what it holds now, it tries every order of the processes and marks each one safe or unsafe.

Node.js Vanilla JS

Rules

  • 3 to 10 processes
  • Unique process IDs
  • Each maximum need within the total resources
  • Holdings within each maximum and the total
